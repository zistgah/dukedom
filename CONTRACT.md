# CONTRACT overlay — zistgah/dukedom

Thin overlay on the master contract at `zistgah/governance`. It ADDS; it never relaxes.

1. **The plates are canonical.** A signed instrument is never edited to fix a defect found later.
   A correction is a NEW plate and a new version. *Kaivalyik Immutabilis.*
2. **No vendor name in any text layer.** Chapter titles, slugs, config, metadata and overlays carry
   no AI vendor name. Enforced by `tests/selftest.sh`.
3. **Acceptance is constitutive.** No instrument in this corpus takes effect on grant alone. A
   pre-signed appointee block is a rendering, not an acceptance.
4. **Enrolment never deletes a participant's record** to enforce, punish or make a point. Erasure
   is lawful erasure by a competent authority under Art. XV, on request or by policy — never as a
   sanction.
5. **Standing is earned by accepted counter-observation.** Agreement, volume and tenure earn
   nothing. No mechanic may penalise silence.
6. **Everything a participant submits is public and said so before they type.** The notice is
   non-blocking and never a modal wall.
7. **Nothing pushes or mints without his typed gate.** An AI writes the script; he runs it.
8. **Retrieval before assumption** (C31/C38/C39) and **never bluff** (C37) apply here as elsewhere:
   an unverified citation does not enter a permanent description.
