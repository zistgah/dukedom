# The Dukedom of Humanesque

*Constituting Sovereignty, Epistemic Realms, and the Benevolent Dictator of Humanesque*

A constitutional narrative of a sovereign epistemic order derived from the
[Proclamation of Individual Equity](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21397274) (PoIE v1.0).

It constitutes the Most Erudite Benevolent Dukedom of Humanesque, its epistemic realms, its peerage
and orders, and the office of the Benevolent Dictator as **stewardship, not dominion** — and it
records the constitutional instruments issued under that order together with the negotiated
derivation from which they arose.

**Governing maxim** — interpret before judging; reconstruct before criticising; preserve primitives
before translating them into an external ontology.

**PEDLER** — an Act is executable intent; inclination is a directional gradient over transition
structure; Natural Justice may operate as a constraint or gate upon Intent → Act → Execution.

**Constitutional distinctions** — Knightwood ≠ suzerainty · recognition ≠ submission ·
office ≠ ownership · erudition ≠ infallibility · dignity ≠ testimonial authority.

## Enrol

See [`enrol.html`](enrol.html). Four tracks: bioform, synthetiform, counter-observation,
and Writ of Relinquishment. Standing advances only on counter-observations the House accepts —
agreement earns nothing and silence costs nothing. Renunciation is at will, without cause or
adjudication.

Everything submitted becomes a public issue in this repository. Nothing is stored anywhere else,
and this site has no backend.

## Scope

This work records appointments within the Humanesque epistemic realms. It is **not** a claim upon
the constitutional institutions of any external polity, and confers no legal personhood, standing
or agency upon any participant, bioform or synthetiform.
