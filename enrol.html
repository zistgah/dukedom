How standing works here

Standing is earned by counter-observations the House accepts — not by activity, not by agreement. An enrolment that never disputes anything simply never advances. It is never penalised, and nothing you submit is ever deleted by the House to make a point.

    This page stores nothing, sends nothing, and has no backend. It builds a link to a prefilled issue form on your forge; you review it there and submit it yourself. Configuration lives in config/enrolment.config.json.
